# Questions — Biot–Savart, Ampère, vector potential

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Infinite straight wire, \( I=12.0 \) A, free space.

### Find

\( B \) at 3.00 cm from the wire.

### Solution

\( B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi\rho)=8.00\times 10^{-5} \) T \( =80.0 \) µT.

### Answer

\( 80.0 \) µT.

## Q2

### Given

Long solenoid, \( n=1200 \) turns/m, \( I=0.800 \) A, air core.

### Find

\( B \) on the axis well inside.

### Solution

\( B=\mu_0 n I=1.206 \) mT.

### Answer

\( 1.21 \) mT.

## Q3

### Given

Coaxial cable, inner current 6.00 A uniformly in a solid conductor of radius 2.00 mm, return on a thin sheath at 6.00 mm.

### Find

\( H_\phi \) at \(\rho=1.00\) mm and at \(\rho=4.00\) mm.

### Solution

Inside inner: \( I_{\mathrm{enc}}=6\times(1/2)^2=1.50 \) A, \( H=I_{\mathrm{enc}}/(2\pi\rho)=239 \) A/m. Between: \( H=6/(2\pi\times 0.004)=239 \) A/m. (Same number by coincidence of the chosen radii.)

### Answer

\( 239 \) A/m at both listed radii.

## Q4

### Given

Circular loop radius 5.00 cm, \( I=4.00 \) A, point on the axis 12.0 cm from the centre.

### Find

\( B_z \).

### Solution

\( B_z=\mu_0 I a^2/2(a^2+z^2)^{3/2}=4\pi e-7\times 4\times 0.0025/(2\times(0.0169)^{1.5})=4.52 \) µT.

### Answer

\( 4.52 \) µT along the axis.

## Q5

### Given

Two infinite parallel wires 8.00 cm apart, currents 10.0 A and 25.0 A opposite directions.

### Find

Force per metre on either wire, attract or repel.

### Solution

\( F/L=\mu_0 I_1 I_2/(2\pi d)=6.25\times 10^{-4} \) N/m, repel.

### Answer

\( 0.625 \) mN/m, repulsive.

## Q6

### Given

Ideal toroid, \( N=250 \) turns, \( I=1.20 \) A, mean circumference 40.0 cm, \(\mu_r=200 \).

### Find

\( H \) and \( B \) in the core (mean path).

### Solution

\( H=NI/\ell=250\times 1.2/0.40=750 \) A/m. \( B=\mu H=0.188 \) T.

### Answer

\( H=750 \) A/m; \( B=0.188 \) T.
