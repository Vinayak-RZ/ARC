# Questions — Boundary conditions, capacitance

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Parallel-plate capacitor, \( A=200 \) cm², \( d=0.50 \) mm, mica \(\varepsilon_r=5.0\), neglect fringing.

### Find

\( C \).

### Solution

\( C=\varepsilon_r\varepsilon_0 A/d=5\times 8.854e-12\times 0.020/5e-4=1.77 \) nF.

### Answer

\( 1.77 \) nF.

## Q2

### Given

Coax, \( a=2.00 \) mm, \( b=8.00 \) mm, \(\varepsilon_r=2.00\), length 12.0 m.

### Find

Total \( C \).

### Solution

\( C'=2\pi\varepsilon/\ln(b/a)=2\pi\times 2\varepsilon_0/\ln 4=80.3 \) pF/m. \( C=0.964 \) nF.

### Answer

\( 0.964 \) nF.

## Q3

### Given

Isolated conducting sphere, radius 5.00 cm, in air, \( V=2.00 \) kV.

### Find

\( C \) and \( Q \).

### Solution

\( C=4\pi\varepsilon_0 a=5.56 \) pF. \( Q=CV=11.1 \) nC.

### Answer

\( C=5.56 \) pF; \( Q=11.1 \) nC.

## Q4

### Given

Just outside a conductor in free space, \(\rho_S=20.0\) nC/m².

### Find

\( E \) immediately outside.

### Solution

\( E=\rho_S/\varepsilon_0=2.26 \) kV/m, normal.

### Answer

\( 2.26 \) kV/m normal.

## Q5

### Given

Two-layer series dielectric, each thickness 1.00 mm, \( A=100 \) cm², \(\varepsilon_{r1}=2\), \(\varepsilon_{r2}=6\), no free charge on the interface, \( V_{\mathrm{total}}=90.0 \) V.

### Find

\( D \), \( E_1 \), \( E_2 \), and \( C \).

### Solution

\( D \) common. \( V=D(d_1/\varepsilon_1+d_2/\varepsilon_2)=D(0.001)(1/(2\varepsilon_0)+1/(6\varepsilon_0))=D\times 0.001\times 2/(3\varepsilon_0) \). \( D=90\times 3\varepsilon_0/0.002=1.195 \) µC/m². \( E_1=D/\varepsilon_1=67.5 \) kV/m, \( E_2=22.5 \) kV/m. \( Q=DA=11.95 \) nC, \( C=Q/V=133 \) pF.

### Answer

\( D=1.20\,\mu\mathrm{C/m}^2 \); \( E_1=67.5 \) kV/m; \( E_2=22.5 \) kV/m; \( C=133 \) pF.

## Q6

### Given

Linear dielectric \(\varepsilon_r=4.0\), \(\mathbf{E}=100\mathbf{a}_x\) V/m.

### Find

\(\mathbf{P}\) and \(\mathbf{D}\).

### Solution

\(\mathbf{D}=\varepsilon\mathbf{E}=4\varepsilon_0\times 100=3.54\mathbf{a}_x\) nC/m². \(\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{D}-\varepsilon_0\mathbf{E}=3\varepsilon_0\times 100=2.66\mathbf{a}_x\) nC/m². Also \(\mathbf{P}=\varepsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf{E}\) with \(\chi_e=3\).

### Answer

\( P=2.66 \) nC/m²; \( D=3.54 \) nC/m², both in \( +\mathbf{a}_x \).
