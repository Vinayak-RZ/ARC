# Questions — Telegrapher, VSWR, reflection

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Lossless line \( Z_0=50.0\,\Omega \), \( Z_L=150\,\Omega \).

### Find

\(\Gamma_L\) and VSWR.

### Solution

\(\Gamma=(150-50)/(150+50)=0.500 \). \( s=(1.5)/(0.5)=3.00 \).

### Answer

\( \Gamma=0.500 \); VSWR \( =3.00 \).

## Q2

### Given

Lossless line, \(\ell=\lambda/4\), \( Z_0=75.0\,\Omega \), \( Z_L=300\,\Omega \).

### Find

\( Z_{\mathrm{in}} \).

### Solution

\( Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_0^2/Z_L=18.75\,\Omega \).

### Answer

\( 18.8\,\Omega \).

## Q3

### Given

Lossless line, \(\ell=\lambda/2\), \( Z_0=50\,\Omega \), \( Z_L=20+j35\,\Omega \).

### Find

\( Z_{\mathrm{in}} \).

### Solution

Half-wave repeats the load: \( Z_{\mathrm{in}}=20+j35\,\Omega \).

### Answer

\( 20+j35\,\Omega \).

## Q4

### Given

Shorted lossless stub, \( Z_0=50.0\,\Omega \), \(\ell=\lambda/8 \).

### Find

\( Z_{\mathrm{in}} \).

### Solution

\( Z_{\mathrm{in}}=j Z_0\tan(\pi/4)=j50.0\,\Omega \).

### Answer

\( j50.0\,\Omega \).

## Q5

### Given

Peak forward voltage \( |V_0^+|=8.00 \) V on a lossless 50.0 Ω line, \( |\Gamma_L|=0.250 \).

### Find

Forward power and net power delivered to the load.

### Solution

\( P^+=|V_0^+|^2/(2Z_0)=0.640 \) W. \( P_{\mathrm{net}}=P^+(1-|\Gamma|^2)=0.640\times 0.9375=0.600 \) W.

### Answer

\( 0.640 \) W forward; \( 0.600 \) W to the load.

## Q6

### Given

Air-filled coax, \( a=1.50 \) mm, \( b=4.80 \) mm, \(\mu=\mu_0\), \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0\). Use \( Z_0=(60)\ln(b/a) \).

### Find

\( Z_0 \).

### Solution

\( \ln(4.80/1.50)=\ln 3.20=1.163 \). \( Z_0=69.8\,\Omega \).

### Answer

\( 69.8\,\Omega \).
