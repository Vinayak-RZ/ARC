# Questions — Coulomb, Gauss, potential, Poisson

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Point charges \( q_1=3.00 \) nC at the origin and \( q_2=-3.00 \) nC at \( x=0.400 \) m, free space.

### Find

\(\mathbf{E}\) at \( (0.200,0,0) \) m.

### Solution

Each charge is 0.200 m away. Magnitudes \( E=9e9\times 3e-9/0.04=675 \) V/m each. Both point in \( -\mathbf{a}_x \) at the midpoint (the positive charge pushes \( +x \) wait: test field at midpoint: \( q_1>0 \) gives \( +x \) field, \( q_2<0 \) (at +x) attracts a positive test to +x, so also \( +x \). Both \( +\mathbf{a}_x \). Total \( 1350\mathbf{a}_x \) V/m.

### Answer

\( 1.35\,\mathrm{kV/m}\ \mathbf{a}_x \).

## Q2

### Given

Infinite line \(\rho_L=40.0\) nC/m on the z-axis, free space.

### Find

\( E \) at \(\rho=0.250\) m.

### Solution

\( E=\rho_L/(2\pi\varepsilon_0\rho)=40e-9/(2\pi\times 8.854e-12\times 0.25)=2.88 \) kV/m.

### Answer

\( 2.88 \) kV/m radial.

## Q3

### Given

Uniform sphere of charge \( Q=12.0 \) nC, radius \( a=3.00 \) cm, free space.

### Find

\( E \) at \( r=2.00 \) cm (inside) and \( r=6.00 \) cm (outside).

### Solution

Outside \( E=9e9\times 12e-9/0.06^2=30.0 \) kV/m. Inside \( E=(Q r)/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 a^3)=9e9\times 12e-9\times 0.02/0.03^3=40.0 \) kV/m.

### Answer

Inside \( 40.0 \) kV/m; outside \( 30.0 \) kV/m.

## Q4

### Given

Coaxial cylinders, inner radius 1.00 cm at 20.0 V, outer 4.00 cm at 0 V, Laplace, \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0\).

### Find

\( V \) and \( E_\rho \) at \(\rho=2.00\) cm.

### Solution

\( V(\rho)=V_a\ln(b/\rho)/\ln(b/a)=20\ln(4/2)/\ln 4=20\ln 2/(2\ln 2)=10.0 \) V. \( E=V_a/(\rho\ln(b/a))=20/(0.02\ln 4)=721 \) V/m.

### Answer

\( V=10.0 \) V; \( E=721 \) V/m radially inward (since \( V \) decreases with \(\rho\)).

## Q5

### Given

Point charge \( 6.00 \) nC, 5.00 cm above a grounded conducting plane.

### Find

Force on the charge.

### Solution

Image \( -6 \) nC, 10.0 cm away. \( F=9e9\times 36e-18/0.10^2=32.4 \) µN toward the plane.

### Answer

\( 32.4\,\mu\mathrm{N} \) toward the plane.

## Q6

### Given

Free-space energy \( W=\frac{\varepsilon_0}{2}\int E^2\mathrm{d}v \). A uniform field 400 V/m fills a volume 0.0100 m³ (ignore fringing, as a model).

### Find

Stored energy.

### Solution

\( W=\frac12\times 8.854e-12\times 160000\times 0.01=7.08 \) nJ.

### Answer

\( 7.08 \) nJ.
