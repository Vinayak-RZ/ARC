# Questions — Rectangular waveguide cutoff, Hertzian dipole intro

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Air-filled rectangular waveguide, \( a=2.00 \) cm, \( b=1.00 \) cm.

### Find

Cutoff frequencies of TE10 and TE20.

### Solution

\( f_{c10}=c/(2a)=7.50 \) GHz. \( f_{c20}=c/a=15.0 \) GHz.

### Answer

\( 7.50 \) GHz; \( 15.0 \) GHz.

## Q2

### Given

The guide of Q1, operation at 10.0 GHz, air-filled, TE10.

### Find

\(\lambda_g\) and \( v_p/c \).

### Solution

\( f_c/f=0.750 \), \(\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}=0.661 \). \(\lambda_0=3.00 \) cm, \(\lambda_g=4.54 \) cm. \( v_p/c=1/0.661=1.51 \).

### Answer

\( \lambda_g=4.54 \) cm; \( v_p=1.51 c \).

## Q3

### Given

Same as Q2.

### Find

Whether 10.0 GHz is single-mode (only TE10 above cutoff among TE10, TE20, TE01, TM11).

### Solution

TE10: 7.50 GHz cutoff (propagating). TE20: 15 GHz (cut off). TE01: \( c/(2b)=15.0 \) GHz (cut off). TM11: \( f_c=(c/2)\sqrt{1/a^2+1/b^2}=16.8 \) GHz (cut off). Single-mode TE10.

### Answer

Yes, only TE10 propagates.

## Q4

### Given

Hertzian dipole \(\ell=1.50 \) cm, \( f=50.0 \) MHz, \(\lambda=c/f\).

### Find

\( \ell/\lambda \) and radiation resistance \( R_r=80\pi^2(\ell/\lambda)^2 \).

### Solution

\(\lambda=6.00 \) m, \(\ell/\lambda=0.00250 \), \( R_r=80\pi^2\times 6.25\times 10^{-6}=4.93 \) mΩ.

### Answer

\( 0.00250 \); \( 4.93 \) mΩ.

## Q5

### Given

Hertzian dipole, peak current 2.00 A, \( R_r=0.200\,\Omega \).

### Find

Radiated average power \( \frac12 I_0^2 R_r \).

### Solution

\( P=0.5\times 4\times 0.200=0.400 \) W.

### Answer

\( 0.400 \) W.

## Q6

### Given

Far-field \( |E_\theta|\propto\sin\theta \). Half-power directions where \(\sin^2\theta=1/2 \).

### Find

Those \(\theta\) in \( (0,\pi) \).

### Solution

\(\sin\theta=1/\sqrt{2}\), \(\theta=45^\circ\) and \( 135^\circ \).

### Answer

\( 45^\circ \) and \( 135^\circ \).
