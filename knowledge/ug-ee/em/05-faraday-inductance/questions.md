# Questions — Faraday, Lenz, inductance

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

A 50-turn coil, area 12.0 cm², uniform \( B=0.250\cos(400 t) \) T normal to the coil.

### Find

The induced emf \(\mathcal{E}(t)\).

### Solution

\(\Phi_1=BA=3.00\times 10^{-4}\cos(400t)\). \(\mathcal{E}=-N\mathrm{d}\Phi_1/\mathrm{d}t=50\times 3e-4\times 400\sin(400t)=6.00\sin(400t) \) V.

### Answer

\( 6.00\sin(400 t) \) V.

## Q2

### Given

Air solenoid, \( N=300 \), length 15.0 cm, cross-section 5.00 cm².

### Find

\( L \).

### Solution

\( L=\mu_0 N^2 A/\ell=4\pi e-7\times 9e4\times 5e-4/0.15=377 \) µH.

### Answer

\( 377 \) µH.

## Q3

### Given

Sliding bar, \( B=0.60 \) T into the page, rail spacing 20.0 cm, \( v=3.00 \) m/s, circuit resistance 0.40 \(\Omega\).

### Find

Emf, current, and magnetic force on the bar.

### Solution

\(\mathcal{E}=B\ell v=0.360 \) V. \( I=0.900 \) A. \( F=BI\ell=0.108 \) N opposing the motion.

### Answer

\( 0.360 \) V; \( 0.900 \) A; \( 0.108 \) N opposing.

## Q4

### Given

\( L_1=8.00 \) mH, \( L_2=2.00 \) mH, \( k=0.750 \).

### Find

\( M \) and the series-aiding equivalent inductance.

### Solution

\( M=k\sqrt{L_1 L_2}=3.00 \) mH. \( L_{\mathrm{eq}}=L_1+L_2+2M=16.0 \) mH.

### Answer

\( M=3.00 \) mH; \( L_{\mathrm{eq}}=16.0 \) mH.

## Q5

### Given

Coax inductance per metre, \( a=0.50 \) mm, \( b=3.50 \) mm, \(\mu=\mu_0\), external only.

### Find

\( L' \).

### Solution

\( L'=(\mu_0/2\pi)\ln(b/a)=389 \) nH/m.

### Answer

\( 389 \) nH/m.

## Q6

### Given

Transformer formula \( E_{\mathrm{rms}}=4.44 f N B_m A \), \( f=60.0 \) Hz, \( N=80 \), \( B_m=0.90 \) T, \( A=25.0 \) cm².

### Find

RMS induced voltage.

### Solution

\( E=4.44\times 60\times 80\times 0.90\times 0.0025=47.95 \) V.

### Answer

\( 48.0 \) V.
