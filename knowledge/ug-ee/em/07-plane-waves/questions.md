# Questions — Uniform plane wave, Poynting, polarization

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

A 100 MHz uniform plane wave in free space.

### Find

\(\lambda\), \(\beta\), and \( v_p \).

### Solution

\(\lambda=c/f=3.00\) m. \(\beta=2\pi/\lambda=2.09\) rad/m. \( v_p=c=3.00\times 10^8 \) m/s.

### Answer

\( 3.00 \) m; \( 2.09 \) rad/m; \( 3.00\times 10^8 \) m/s.

## Q2

### Given

Peak \( E_0=5.00 \) V/m, lossless \(\eta=377\,\Omega\), \( +z \) TEM wave.

### Find

Peak \( H \) and average Poynting magnitude.

### Solution

\( H_0=E_0/\eta=13.3 \) mA/m. \( S_{\mathrm{av}}=E_0^2/(2\eta)=33.2 \) mW/m².

### Answer

\( 13.3 \) mA/m; \( 33.2 \) mW/m².

## Q3

### Given

Lossless dielectric \(\varepsilon_r=9.00\), \(\mu=\mu_0\), \( f=1.00 \) GHz, \( E_0=1.00 \) V/m peak.

### Find

\(\eta\), \(\lambda\), and \( S_{\mathrm{av}} \).

### Solution

\(\eta=\eta_0/3=125.7\,\Omega \). \( v_p=c/3 \), \(\lambda=0.100 \) m. \( S_{\mathrm{av}}=1^2/(2\times 125.7)=3.98 \) mW/m².

### Answer

\( 126\,\Omega \); \( 10.0 \) cm; \( 3.98 \) mW/m².

## Q4

### Given

Copper \(\sigma=5.80\times 10^7\) S/m, \( f=10.0 \) kHz, \(\mu=\mu_0\).

### Find

Skin depth \(\delta\).

### Solution

\(\delta=\sqrt{2/(\omega\mu\sigma)}=\sqrt{2/(2\pi\times 10^4\times 4\pi e-7\times 5.8e7)}=0.661 \) mm.

### Answer

\( 0.661 \) mm.

## Q5

### Given

\(\tilde{\mathbf{E}}=(2\mathbf{a}_x-j2\mathbf{a}_y)e^{-j\beta z}\) V/m in free space.

### Find

Polarization (type and whether equal-amplitude quadrature).

### Solution

Equal magnitudes, \( E_y \) lags \( E_x \) by 90° (the \(-j\) on \( y \)). Circularly polarized.

### Answer

Circular (equal-amplitude quadrature).

## Q6

### Given

Attenuation constant \(\alpha=0.0500\) Np/m, distance 40.0 m, lossless \(\eta\) otherwise only this \(\alpha\). Incident peak \( E \) at \( z=0 \) is 10.0 V/m.

### Find

Peak \( E \) at 40.0 m and the power-density ratio \( S(40)/S(0) \).

### Solution

\( E=10 e^{-0.05\times 40}=10 e^{-2}=1.353 \) V/m. Power density \(\propto |E|^2\), ratio \( e^{-4}=0.0183 \).

### Answer

\( 1.35 \) V/m; ratio \( 0.0183 \) (\(-17.4\) dB).
