# Questions — Laws of illumination, electric heating

Original numbers.

## Q1

### Given

A point source of 1200 cd, 6.0 m above a horizontal working plane. Find illuminance at a point on the plane 8.0 m horizontally from the vertical through the lamp. Lambert cosine on the plane; isotropic source.

### Find

Distance, \(\cos\theta\), and illuminance.

### Solution

\[
d=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10.0\,\mathrm{m},\qquad\cos\theta=6/10=0.60,
\]
\[
E=\frac{1200}{10^2}\times 0.60=7.20\,\mathrm{lx}.
\]

### Answer

\(7.20\,\mathrm{lx}\)

## Q2

### Given

Hall \(20\,\mathrm{m}\times 12\,\mathrm{m}\), required \(E=250\,\mathrm{lx}\), lamps of \(2800\,\mathrm{lm}\) each, \(\mathrm{UF}=0.65\), \(\mathrm{MF}=0.75\).

### Find

Number of lamps (round up to the next integer).

### Solution

\[
N=\frac{250\times 20\times 12}{2800\times 0.65\times 0.75}=\frac{60000}{1365}=43.96.
\]

Use \(N=44\).

### Answer

\(44\) lamps

## Q3

### Given

Isotropic lamp, total flux \(6283\,\mathrm{lm}\).

### Find

MSCP.

### Solution

\[
\mathrm{MSCP}=\frac{6283}{4\pi}=\frac{6283}{12.566}=500.0\,\mathrm{cd}.
\]

### Answer

\(500\,\mathrm{cd}\)

## Q4

### Given

Resistance oven, 40 kg aluminium, \(c=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}\), 25 °C to 225 °C, \(\eta=0.80\), element 12 kW.

### Find

Time.

### Solution

\[
Q=40\times 900\times 200=7.20\times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{J},\qquad t=\frac{Q}{\eta P}=\frac{7.20\times 10^{6}}{0.80\times 12000}=750\,\mathrm{s}=12.5\,\mathrm{min}.
\]

### Answer

\(750\,\mathrm{s}\) (\(12.5\,\mathrm{min}\))

## Q5

### Given

Dielectric heating: \(\varepsilon_r=4.0\), \(\tan\delta=0.040\), \(E=800\,\mathrm{V/cm}=8.00\times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}\), \(f=20\,\mathrm{MHz}\), volume \(0.012\,\mathrm{m}^3\). \(P=\omega\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r E^2\tan\delta\times\mathrm{Vol}\), \(\varepsilon_0=8.85\times 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}\).

### Find

Power absorbed.

### Solution

\[
\omega=2\pi\times 20\times 10^{6}=1.257\times 10^{8},
\]
\[
P=1.257\times 10^{8}\times 8.85\times 10^{-12}\times 4.0\times (8.00\times 10^{4})^2\times 0.040\times 0.012
\]
\[
=1.257\times 10^{8}\times 8.85\times 10^{-12}\times 4\times 6.40\times 10^{9}\times 0.040\times 0.012=1.37\times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W}.
\]

### Answer

\(13.7\,\mathrm{kW}\)

## Q6

### Given

Skin depth in a non-magnetic conductor, \(\rho=8.0\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}\), \(\mu=\mu_0=4\pi\times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}\), \(f=50\,\mathrm{kHz}\). \(\delta=\sqrt{\rho/(\pi f\mu)}\).

### Find

Skin depth.

### Solution

\[
\pi f\mu=\pi\times 5.0\times 10^{4}\times 4\pi\times 10^{-7}=1.974\times 10^{-1},
\]
\[
\delta=\sqrt{8.0\times 10^{-8}/0.1974}=\sqrt{4.053\times 10^{-7}}=6.37\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}=0.637\,\mathrm{mm}.
\]

### Answer

\(0.637\,\mathrm{mm}\)
