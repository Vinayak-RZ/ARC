# Questions — Speed-time curves, mechanics of train

Original numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Trapezoidal run: \(\alpha=0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}^2\), \(\beta=0.80\,\mathrm{m/s}^2\), \(v_m=18.0\,\mathrm{m/s}\), total running time \(T=70\,\mathrm{s}\). Distance \(D=v_m T-\frac12 v_m^2(1/\alpha+1/\beta)\).

### Find

Acceleration time, braking time, free-run time, and distance.

### Solution

\[
t_1=18/0.50=36.0\,\mathrm{s},\qquad t_3=18/0.80=22.5\,\mathrm{s},\qquad t_2=70-36-22.5=11.5\,\mathrm{s},
\]
\[
D=18\times 70-\frac12\times 324\times(2.00+1.25)=1260-162\times 3.25=1260-526.5=733.5\,\mathrm{m}.
\]

### Answer

\(t_1=36.0\,\mathrm{s}\), \(t_3=22.5\,\mathrm{s}\), \(t_2=11.5\,\mathrm{s}\), \(D=734\,\mathrm{m}\)

## Q2

### Given

Train mass \(m=250\,\mathrm{t}\), rotary allowance \(\gamma=0.10\), acceleration \(0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}^2\) on a level track, resistance \(60\,\mathrm{N/t}\) of actual mass.

### Find

Tractive effort.

### Solution

\[
m_{\mathrm{eff}}=250\times 10^{3}\times 1.10=2.75\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kg},
\]
\[
F_{\mathrm{acc}}=2.75\times 10^{5}\times 0.40=1.10\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N},
\]
\[
R=60\times 250=1.50\times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N},\qquad F=1.25\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}=125\,\mathrm{kN}.
\]

### Answer

\(125\,\mathrm{kN}\)

## Q3

### Given

The train of Q2 on an up-gradient of 1 in 80 (\(i=1/80\)). Same \(\alpha\) and resistance. \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}^2\).

### Find

Tractive effort.

### Solution

\[
F_g=250\times 10^{3}\times 9.81/80=3.066\times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N},
\]
\[
F=1.10\times 10^{5}+1.50\times 10^{4}+3.07\times 10^{4}=1.557\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}=156\,\mathrm{kN}.
\]

### Answer

\(156\,\mathrm{kN}\)

## Q4

### Given

Driven (adhesive) mass \(120\,\mathrm{t}\), \(\mu=0.20\), \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}^2\).

### Find

Maximum starting tractive effort from adhesion.

### Solution

\[
F_{\max}=\mu m g=0.20\times 120\times 10^{3}\times 9.81=2.354\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}=235\,\mathrm{kN}.
\]

### Answer

\(235\,\mathrm{kN}\)

## Q5

### Given

Level trapezoid, \(m_{\mathrm{eff}}=2.75\times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kg}\), \(v_m=18\,\mathrm{m/s}\), \(D=733.5\,\mathrm{m}\), constant resistance \(R=15.0\,\mathrm{kN}\), no regeneration (braking energy dumped). Energy at wheels \(W=\tfrac12 m_{\mathrm{eff}} v_m^2+R D\).

### Find

Energy and specific energy in Wh/(t·km) using actual mass \(250\,\mathrm{t}\).

### Solution

\[
\tfrac12 m_{\mathrm{eff}} v_m^2=0.5\times 2.75\times 10^{5}\times 324=4.455\times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J},
\]
\[
R D=15000\times 733.5=1.100\times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J},\qquad W=5.555\times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}=15.43\,\mathrm{kWh}.
\]
\[
D=0.7335\,\mathrm{km},\qquad \frac{15.43\times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Wh}}{250\,\mathrm{t}\times 0.7335\,\mathrm{km}}=84.1\,\mathrm{Wh/(t\cdot km)}.
\]

### Answer

\(15.4\,\mathrm{kWh}\); \(84.1\,\mathrm{Wh/(t\cdot km)}\)

## Q6

### Given

Trapezoid of Q1. Average running speed \(D/T\). Schedule speed with dwell \(20\,\mathrm{s}\) at the stop, one inter-station run.

### Find

Average running speed and schedule speed.

### Solution

\[
v_{\mathrm{avg}}=733.5/70=10.48\,\mathrm{m/s}=37.7\,\mathrm{km/h},
\]
\[
v_{\mathrm{sch}}=733.5/(70+20)=8.15\,\mathrm{m/s}=29.3\,\mathrm{km/h}.
\]

### Answer

\(10.5\,\mathrm{m/s}\) running; \(8.15\,\mathrm{m/s}\) schedule
