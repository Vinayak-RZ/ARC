# Communication systems for EEE — syllabus union

Bound: `docs/curriculum-map.md` (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay). Signals-pack modulation intro is assumed; this pack is the EEE *communication systems* elective.

This pack is analog modulation (AM/DSB/SSB/FM and an SNR sketch) then digital baseband (sampling, PCM, line codes, Nyquist ISI). Depth is coursework (assignment, lab numerical, exam-style), not a coding-theory course, OFDM thesis, or a live RF licence.

## Units

- `01-analog-modulation` — AM, DSB, SSB, FM, SNR intro
- `02-digital-pcm-line-codes` — Sampling, PCM, line codes, ISI intro

## Outcomes

After this pack a UG EEE student can compute AM total and sideband power from \(\mu\); state DSB versus SSB bandwidth; apply Carson’s rule and the modulation index \(\beta\); size PCM bit rate and SQNR; name NRZ/RZ/AMI/Manchester spectra at UG depth; and use the raised-cosine bandwidth \((1+\alpha)R_b/2\).
