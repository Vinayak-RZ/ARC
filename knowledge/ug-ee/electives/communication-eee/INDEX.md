# Communication systems for EEE

Undergraduate handbook for the Arc knowledge pack `communication-eee` (group `electives-embed`). Units match `knowledge/COVERAGE.yaml`. Each unit has `notes.md`, `questions.md`, and `sources.md`. Original notes only; no `oer/` copies in this pack.

Bound: `SYLLABUS.md` and `docs/curriculum-map.md`. Pack path: `knowledge/ug-ee/electives/communication-eee`. Checker: `python scripts/check_knowledge_tree.py --pack electives-embed`.
