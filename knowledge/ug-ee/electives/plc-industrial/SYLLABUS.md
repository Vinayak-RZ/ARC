# PLC and industrial automation (UG) — syllabus union

Bound: `docs/curriculum-map.md` (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay). This is an EEE *elective* at study depth, not a controls-room operator course.

This pack is PLC scan and ladder (timers/counters) then sensors, analog IO, and interlocks. Depth is coursework (assignment, lab numerical, exam-style). **Out of this pack and out of the product:** live PLC / plant actuation.

## Units

- `01-ladder-scan-cycle` — Scan cycle, ladder, timers/counters
- `02-sensors-actuators-ug` — Digital/analog IO, interlocks (study-level)

## Outcomes

After this pack a UG EEE student can describe the input–logic–output scan; size TON elapsed time versus scan period; distinguish TON/TOF/RTO and CTU/CTD; scale 4–20 mA and 0–10 V; name sinking versus sourcing; and write a study-level interlock (guard, e-stop as NC fail-safe).
