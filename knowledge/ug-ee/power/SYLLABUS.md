# Power systems — syllabus union

Bound: docs/curriculum-map.md (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay).

Units:

- `01-per-unit-system` — Base change, single-line diagrams
- `02-transmission-line-models` — Short/medium/long lines, ABCD
- `03-load-flow` — Ybus, Gauss-Seidel, Newton-Raphson intro
- `04-symmetrical-faults` — Zbus, bolted three-phase faults
- `05-unsymmetrical-faults-sequence` — Sequence networks, LG/LL/LLG
- `06-power-system-stability` — Swing equation, equal-area
- `07-economic-operation` — IC, B-coefficients, penalty factor
- `08-cables-insulators-grounding` — Cables, string efficiency, grounding
- `09-distribution-systems` — Feeders, radial vs ring, voltage drop
- `10-protection-overview` — Zones, CTs/VTs, overcurrent intro
- `11-hvdc-facts-intro` — HVDC and FACTS at UG level
- `12-reactive-power-voltage-control` — Q-V, shunt/series compensation
