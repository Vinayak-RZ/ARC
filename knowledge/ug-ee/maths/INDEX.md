# Mathematics for EE

Undergraduate mathematics as used in electrical engineering cores: complex arithmetic and phasors, linear ODEs, Laplace and Fourier analysis, matrices, vector calculus, probability of noise, numerical methods, Z-transforms, and inversion by partial fractions and residues. Not a pure-mathematics degree.

Bound: `docs/curriculum-map.md` (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay). Pack path: `knowledge/ug-ee/maths`. Checker: `python scripts/check_knowledge_tree.py --pack maths`.
