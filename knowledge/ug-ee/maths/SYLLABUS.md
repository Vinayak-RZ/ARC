Bound: docs/curriculum-map.md (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay).

Units:

- `01-complex-numbers-phasors-math` — Complex arithmetic and Euler
- `02-ode-linear-systems` — Linear ODEs and constant-coefficient systems
- `03-laplace-transforms` — Laplace tables, theorems, inverse
- `04-fourier-series-transform-math` — Fourier analysis as maths
- `05-linear-algebra-ee` — Matrices, eigenvalues, quadratic forms
- `06-vector-calculus` — grad, div, curl, integral theorems
- `07-probability-random-variables` — RV, expectation, Gaussian noise intro
- `08-numerical-methods-ee` — Root finding, integration, linear solves
- `09-z-transform-math` — Z as a transform pair
- `10-partial-fractions-residues` — Partial fractions and residues
