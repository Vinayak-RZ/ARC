Bound: docs/curriculum-map.md (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay).

Units:

- `02-transformers-equivalent-circuit` — Ideal and practical single-phase transformer
- `03-transformer-tests-three-phase` — OC/SC, regulation, three-phase connections
- `04-dc-generators` — Generated emf, types, characteristics
- `05-dc-motors` — Torque, speed control, starters
- `06-induction-machines` — Equivalent circuit, torque-slip
- `07-induction-starting-speed-control` — Starting, cascade, V/f intro
- `08-synchronous-generators` — Pitch, winding factor, OCC/SCC, regulation
- `09-synchronous-motors` — V-curves, hunting, starting
- `10-special-machines` — Stepper, BLDC, reluctance, servo intro
- `11-per-unit-machine-tests` — Per-unit machines and standard tests
