# Signals and systems — syllabus union

Bound: docs/curriculum-map.md (IITR, NITT, AICTE-family, MIT/Berkeley, GATE overlay).

Units:

- `01-signal-classifications` — Continuous/discrete, energy/power, even/odd, periodic
- `02-lti-systems-properties` — Linearity, time-invariance, memory, invertibility
- `03-convolution` — Continuous and discrete convolution
- `04-fourier-series` — CTFS/DTFS and properties
- `05-fourier-transform` — CTFT and properties, Parseval
- `06-laplace-transform-signals` — ROC, unilateral Laplace, system functions
- `07-sampling-reconstruction` — Nyquist, aliasing, reconstruction
- `08-z-transform` — ROC, inverse Z, discrete LTI
- `09-dft-dfs-dtft` — DTFT, DFS, DFT relations
- `10-filters-as-systems` — Ideal filters, distortion, group delay
- `11-bibo-stability-causality` — BIBO, causality, ROC implications
- `12-modulation-intro` — AM/complex exponential modulation for signals courses
