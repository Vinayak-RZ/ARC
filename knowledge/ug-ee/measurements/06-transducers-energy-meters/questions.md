# Questions — Strain, RTD, induction energy meter

Original pedagogical numbers.

## Q1

### Given

Foil gauge \( R=350.0\,\Omega \), \( G_f=2.00 \), axial strain \( 600\,\mu\varepsilon \).

### Find

\( \Delta R \) and the quarter-bridge output for \( V_s=8.00 \) V (equal arms, one active gauge).

### Solution

\( \Delta R=G_f\varepsilon R=2\times 600\times 10^{-6}\times 350=0.420\,\Omega \). \( V_o\approx V_s G_f\varepsilon/4=8\times 2\times 600\times 10^{-6}/4=2.40 \) mV.

### Answer

\( \Delta R=0.420\,\Omega \); \( V_o=2.40 \) mV.

## Q2

### Given

Pt100, \( R_0=100.0\,\Omega \), \( \alpha=0.00385\,\mathrm{K}^{-1} \), linear model, \( R=107.7\,\Omega \).

### Find

Temperature in °C.

### Solution

\( R=R_0(1+\alpha T)\Rightarrow T=(R/R_0-1)/\alpha=(1.077-1)/0.00385=20.0 \) °C.

### Answer

\( 20.0 \) °C.

## Q3

### Given

Type-K thermocouple, linear \( 41.0\,\mu\mathrm{V}/°\mathrm{C} \), cold junction 0 °C, measured EMF 3.28 mV.

### Find

Hot-junction temperature.

### Solution

\( \Delta T=3.28\times 10^{-3}/(41.0\times 10^{-6})=80.0 \) °C. \( T_h=80.0 \) °C.

### Answer

\( 80.0 \) °C.

## Q4

### Given

Induction meter constant \( K=600 \) rev/kWh. A test records 120 revolutions.

### Find

Registered energy in kWh and in Wh.

### Solution

\( E=120/600=0.200 \) kWh \( =200 \) Wh.

### Answer

\( 0.200 \) kWh (\( 200 \) Wh).

## Q5

### Given

Phantom load: voltage coil 240 V, current coil 4.00 A, PF 1.00, duration 5.00 min. Meter constant 800 rev/kWh. Observed 65.0 revolutions.

### Find

True energy, expected revolutions, and percent error (measured versus true).

### Solution

\( t=300 \) s. \( E_{\mathrm{true}}=240\times 4\times 300=288000 \) J \( =80.0 \) Wh \( =0.0800 \) kWh. Expected \( N=0.0800\times 800=64.0 \) rev. Error \( (65-64)/64=+1.56\% \) (fast).

### Answer

True \( 80.0 \) Wh; expected 64.0 rev; error \( +1.56\% \).

## Q6

### Given

Full-bridge, four active gauges, \( G_f=2.10 \), \( V_s=5.00 \) V, \( V_o=4.20 \) mV, ideal \( V_o/V_s=G_f\varepsilon \).

### Find

Strain \( \varepsilon \).

### Solution

\( \varepsilon=V_o/(V_s G_f)=0.0042/(5\times 2.10)=400\,\mu\varepsilon \).

### Answer

\( 400\,\mu\varepsilon \).
